# Datasets

Every dataset of the course is either bundled with a standard Python library or generated inside the notebook with a fixed random seed, so no download or upload is needed.

| Dataset | Source | Used on | Licence or origin |
|---|---|---|---|
| TokenWorld | Defined in the notebooks: a four-token vocabulary with a hand-written reward | Days 1 to 3 | Created for the course |
| Windy Cliff | A 4 x 8 gridworld with stochastic transitions, defined in the notebooks | Days 1 and 2 | Created for the course |
| Synthetic sentence corpus | Generated from word lists with a fixed seed | Days 4 and 5 | Created for the course |
| Preference pairs | Generated from the gold reward with a controllable annotator error | Days 4 and 5 | Created for the course |
