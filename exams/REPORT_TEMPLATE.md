# Report template

## Title

A specific title that names the method and the problem.

## Abstract

About 150 words: Problem, method, main result and its limits.

## 1. Introduction

The problem, why it matters and the question the project answers.

## 2. Related work

Three to six published sources, cited with numbers in square brackets such as [1].

## 3. Data and methods

The data, its origin and licence, every method with its settings and seeds.

## 4. Results

Tables and figures produced by the notebook, each referred to in the text.

## 5. Discussion

What the results show, what they do not show and at least one negative finding.

## 6. Conclusion

The answer to the question of the introduction in a few sentences.

## Statement on tools

Every tool used, including generative AI, and what was checked by hand.

## References

Numbered list in order of first citation, each entry verified against its source.
