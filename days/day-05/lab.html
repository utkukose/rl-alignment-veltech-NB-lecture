Evaluation lab

Part A is an evaluation desk with 400 real outputs of four policies, judges and intervals. Part B reads the intervals of six comparisons. Part C computes a win rate and its interval by hand.

Part A: The evaluation desk

The desk holds 400 real outputs of each of four policies from the notebook, with their gold reward, reward-model score and length. Choose two policies, a judge, a rule for ties and a number of comparisons. The desk pairs outputs, computes the win rate of A over B and a bootstrap interval, and keeps a log of every evaluation you run.
